Dorm Room
Your dorm room is messy after a busy day of classes. Clothes hang over
your desk chair, and your bed is covered with school supplies. A concert
poster on the wall reminds you that the show is tonight, but your ticket
is missing. You remember having it earlier today.

A bookstore receipt lies on your desk. It might help you remember where
you went. The door leads out toward the Campus Quad.
#
Library
The library is quiet except for the sound of turning pages and students
typing. Tall bookshelves surround rows of study tables. You recognize a
table where you stopped earlier today.

This looks like a good place to examine the notebook from the Bookstore.
Something small could easily be hidden between its pages. Outside, the
path leads back toward the Campus Quad.
#
Campus Quad
The Campus Quad is an open grassy space surrounded by classroom buildings.
Students hurry past with backpacks, and colorful flyers advertise campus
events. One flyer lists the starting time for tonight's concert. You need
to find your ticket before it is too late.

Paths lead toward the Dorm Room and Library. The Dining Hall is to the
west, the Student Center is to the east, and the Bookstore is to the south.
#
Dining Hall
The dining hall smells like pizza and fresh french fries. Students fill
the tables, talking about their classes and plans for the evening. Your
friend Addison is sitting at a table with her food.

She looks worried and keeps checking her bag as though something is
missing. Talking to her might help you remember what happened today.
The exit to the east leads back to the Campus Quad.
#
Student Center
The Student Center has comfortable chairs, campus announcements, and a
row of numbered lockers along one wall. Students stop here between
classes to relax and store their belongings.

The lockers are closed, and you cannot tell what is inside them. You will
need to learn which locker to open and find the correct key. The doors
to the west lead back to the Campus Quad.
#
Bookstore
The bookstore is filled with textbooks, notebooks, and college sweatshirts.
A display of pens stands beside the checkout counter. The receipt from
your dorm reminds you that you shopped here earlier today.

A note at the counter says you left carrying someone else's notebook.
It mentions a study table in the Library, which could be your next clue.
The Campus Quad is to the north, and the Parking Garage is to the south.
#
Parking Garage
The parking garage is cool and dim, with rows of cars beneath bright
overhead lights. Footsteps echo across the concrete floor. Your ride to
the concert is waiting, and there is not much time left.

This is where you need to bring your concert ticket before leaving campus.
If you are still missing it, you must keep searching. The path to the
north leads back to the Bookstore.
#
